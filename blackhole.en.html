Calculating the score

Final = round[min(100, basic + advanced + root-cause refund) + extensions + tier adjustment]

Required work is capped at 100, original extensions at 20, and T0 adds 3: the theoretical maximum is 123. Preserve decimals until the final rounding step; break ties with the exact score.

Tier adjustments: T0 +3; T1 and T4 0; T2 −3; T3 −6. Recommendations, reasons and tiers are recorded by the site author. The gold benchmark is a reference, excluded from ranking; other scores of 96 or above are green.

Original extensions · up to 20

Count at most five independent physical phenomena or demonstrations, each worth 0–4: (1) directly usable from the interface; (2) at least one number with a formula or source is within 10%; (3) at least two live-computed key values are within 3% and respond to parameter changes; (4) shares the main scene’s integrator, units or ray-tracing pipeline. Each condition earns one point; failing (1) makes the entire item zero. Required features earn no extension credit.

Attribution, shared causes & fatal errors

Assess the supplied task: contradictions inherent in its data or equations are not charged to the model, and unrequested features are not omissions. A shared root cause must be demonstrated by changing only that cause in a copy and rerunning measurements. Its cascading loss is capped at 10 points for the highest-weight pitfalls, otherwise 6; the excess is refunded. Original files remain intact.

L1 means the main view is unusable. L2 means a core or long-run failure, such as fake geodesic rendering, reproducible crashes, runaway time or a default scene below 5 fps. Fatal errors assign gray T4 (unfinished); the measured score remains uncapped.

Evaluation & performance

Use an Apple M4-class machine, Chrome, offline file://, 1920×1080 and DPR 1. Check physical values and six image-consistency tests, operate controls and extreme parameters, review extensions, and inspect a 10-minute run, two resizes and background recovery. Any tool-limited checks are identified separately. Automated measurements cover 68.5 points; remaining required points are manually verified.

Performance is worth 4: a five-second default-scene average of ≥55 / 40–55 / 25–40 / <25 fps earns 3 / 2 / 1 / 0. Full-resolution performance ≥55 fps or effective adaptive load reduction earns one more. A band achieved only below 0.5 internal scale drops one band. Performance is measured with one work running.
